Her story

From the crown to the camera

Noelani grew up in Las Vegas as a gymnast, a hurdler and a pageant girl. She won Miss Nevada Teen USA, ran track at UNLV, and now tells stories from Nashville, Michigan and everywhere in between.

Early

Born in Altus, Oklahoma. Raised in Las Vegas.

A gymnast from childhood. She still pulls off giants on the bar for fun.

2018

First crown at 14

Named Miss Teen El Tiempo 2019 as a freshman at Faith Lutheran High School, a preliminary to the state pageant.

2019–20

Semifinalist, Miss Nevada Teen USA

Two years in a row. She kept coming back.
