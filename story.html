Her story

From the crown
to the camera.

Born in Altus, Oklahoma and raised in Las Vegas, Noelani grew up competing: on the gymnastics floor, on the hurdles and on the pageant stage. Today she shares faith, fashion and travel with millions of viewers.

Early

A gymnast first

She trained in gymnastics for six years. Ten years later she walked back into a gym and swung giants again; that video passed 750K views.

2018

First crown at 14

Named Miss Teen El Tiempo 2019 as a freshman at Faith Lutheran High School in Las Vegas.

2019–20

Semifinalist, Miss Nevada Teen USA

Two years in a row. She kept coming back.

2021

Miss Nevada Teen USA

Crowned on June 28, 2021, then represented Nevada at Miss Teen USA in Tulsa, Oklahoma that November.

Miss Nevada Teen USA 2021
2022

School record holder

Graduated from Faith Lutheran holding the school record in the 100m hurdles (15.44).

UNLV

A Runnin’ Rebel

Ran hurdles for UNLV Women’s Track & Field and studied business.

2024

Married Jude Barclay

They met in 2023 and married at the start of 2024, then honeymooned, visited Australia and Japan, and went on a mission trip.

2025

The world gets bigger

China, South Korea and Hawaii, with life split between Michigan and Nashville. Her travel videos start reaching millions.

2026

Creator and model

Modeling and live selling for Well Dressed, joining Jude on his first tour, Paris for the first time, and her biggest video yet at 18.7M views.
